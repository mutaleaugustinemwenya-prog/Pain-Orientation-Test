Eight Laws of Pain

The Reorientation Protocol

Pain is not the exception to a well-built life. It is a structural feature of any life lived with genuine engagement. Ahead of you stand eight portals, each built from a law that governs how suffering moves through a human being. You will not escape any of them. You will only learn to pass through with your eyes open. At the end, the same instrument used by The Pain Orientation Test will name where you currently stand.
